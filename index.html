Questions

Is my schedule data private?

Yes. The free health check runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your XER file is never uploaded, transmitted, or stored anywhere. You can disconnect from the internet after the page loads and it still works.

Is this a replacement for a full schedule audit?

No. It's a fast structural read, useful for catching obvious issues before a deeper review. It doesn't recalculate the critical path, compare against a baseline, or check resource loading.

Does this work with anything other than P6?

Right now it reads standard Primavera P6 .xer exports only.

Do I need an account to buy the app?

No. It is a one-time purchase and a direct download. No subscription, no account, no licence key to keep track of.

Does the app upload my schedule anywhere?

No. It runs entirely on your own machine. Nothing is sent to a server, which also means you can use it on confidential work without clearing it with anyone.

Windows says it will not run the app. Is something wrong?

No. Windows shows a "protected your PC" box for any new independent software that has not built up download history yet. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". Nothing else is needed.

Do I need Excel?

The workbook is generated without it. Excel is used to produce the PDF version, so if you do not have Excel you still get the .xlsx and simply do not get the PDF.
